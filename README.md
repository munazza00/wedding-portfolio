# Luxury Wedding Portfolio & Customizable Admin Studio

A premium, culturally versatile wedding portfolio website with motion-driven visuals, an ornate royal crest logo, vibrant ombre palettes (**Powder Pink, Coral Haze, Garden Green, and Warm Taupe**), animated calligraphy handwriting, and an interactive **Admin Studio**.

---

## ✨ Enhancements & Sections

### 1. 🎨 Vibrant Ombre Color Palette
- **Powder Pink (`#F7D6D0`)**: Soft romantic blush glow.
- **Coral Haze (`#E27D60`)**: Rich, vibrant warm coral energy.
- **Garden Green (`#2D5A3E`)**: Deep, lush botanical greenery.
- **Warm Taupe (`#8B7355`)**: Sophisticated earthy silk warmth.
- **Rich Ombre Gradients**: Multi-stop gradient borders, glowing card surfaces, and radiant accents that eliminate flat or washed-out looks.

### 2. 🍃 Motion-Driven Visuals
- **Multi-layered falling petals & botanicals**: Drifting coral and powder pink rose petals, swaying garden green eucalyptus leaves, and twinkling golden stardust particles with 3D tumble animations.
- **Pulsing Ambient Glow Spheres**: Soft, breathing radial gradient orbs in Coral Haze and Garden Green that provide warmth and depth across the canvas.
- **Interactive Confetti**: Celebratory bursts when unlocking the private family album or sending toasts.

### 3. 👑 Royal Crest Monogram Logo
- Hand-crafted SVG royal crest featuring:
  - Botanical laurel wreath intertwined with blooming leaves.
  - Royal crown/diadem motif topping the crest.
  - Interlocking monogram initials (`A & M` or customized).
  - Shimmering metallic gold and coral haze gradients with hover scaling.

### 4. ✒️ Improved Calligraphy Hero Section
- Minimalist royal presentation displaying **only the names of the groom and bride** in calligraphy art.
- Golden fountain pen nib physically traces the cursive curves as ink flows onto the canvas.
- Framed by grand corner botanical line-art arches and an animated calligraphy flourish ribbon.
- Dedicated **"Replay Calligraphy"** button.

### 5. 🌺 Vibrant Pre-Wedding Festivities (Haldi, Mehndi, Sangeet & More)
- High-contrast, festive ombre cards with glowing borders.
- **100% Floral decor & architectural flatlays** (strictly zero couple photos).
- Clear event metadata: Event theme, dress code color chip with swatch preview, hosting family badge (*Groom's Family*, *Bride's Family*, or *Joint Celebration*), date, timing, and venue details.
- Quick filter pills (*All*, *Bride's Side*, *Groom's Side*, *Joint Celebrations*).

### 6. 🏛️ Sacred Venues & Grand Reception
- Dedicated cards for the **Wedding Ceremony (Pheras)** and the **Grand Gala Reception**.
- Features floral mandap arches, crystal chandeliers, timings, directions, attire notes, and direct Google Maps buttons.

### 7. 🔒 Dedicated Family Pictures Vault (Aesthetic Glimpse Removed)
- The public aesthetic glimpse grid has been **completely removed**.
- Replaced by a dedicated, majestic **Heirloom Family Vault** gateway card.
- Clicking **"Enter Passcode to Reveal Pictures"** prompts for a secret PIN (**Default: `royal2025`**, customizable via Admin).
- Unlocking the vault triggers multi-colored confetti and reveals the private family moments, generational blessings, and candid backstage keepsakes with an interactive lightbox.

### 8. 🤝 Wedding Artisans & Event Management Team
- Recognizes event planners, caterers, floral scenographers, lighting and sound engineers, calligraphy artists, and cinematographers with direct Instagram and portfolio links.

### 9. 💌 Love & Blessings Digital Guestbook
- Allows guests to send toasts and heartfelt wishes, rendered with wax seals.

---

## ⚙️ The Admin Studio (Customizable Panel)

Click **"Admin Studio"** in the top navbar or footer to customize everything:
- **🎨 Themes & Palettes**: Switch cultural presets or use direct color pickers for Powder Pink, Coral Haze, Garden Green, Taupe, Gold, and Oxblood with real-time live preview.
- **✒️ Names & Calligraphy**: Change Groom & Bride names, adjust handwriting speed (0.8s to 3.5s), and edit the monogram.
- **🌺 Pre-Wedding Events**: Add, edit, or remove events, themes, dress codes, and hosting sides.
- **🏛️ Venues & Reception**: Update timings, addresses, and floral decor imagery.
- **🔒 Family Passcode**: Update the secret PIN, hint, and family album photos.
- **🤝 Wedding Artisans**: Add or update event managers, caterers, and florists.
- **💾 Backup & Reset**: Export entire setup to a `.json` file, import previous backups, or reset to defaults.
- *All edits are preserved locally in browser storage.*
